What USDT to INR rate is used here?

The task screenshot shows ₹980 income on a ₹24,500 task, which is 4% of the amount. A ₹1,000 amount would give ₹40 at that same percentage. These are arithmetic examples; eligibility and actual amounts depend on current task terms. The 2% bonus ratio is separate.
