Understand the team summary

Commission and Team Recharge are separate labels in the uploaded image. Do not treat either label as an unconditional promise of income. The figures on your account depend on the service’s current terms and recorded activity. If an amount changes or appears incorrect, compare the relevant history entries and dates before asking support for an explanation. Public screenshots are useful for locating a panel, but they cannot verify the balance, eligibility or activity of your own team.

Review an invite link before sharing it

The Invite Code area displays a registration address with a code parameter and a copy button. Use the link generated by your own account when you want to share an invitation. Check that the destination and invite code are complete after copying. The supplied screenshot contains an undefined code value, so that visible example should not be copied as a working invitation. This website’s registration guide links to the separate registration destination already supplied for the project.

Read team details and current conditions

The Team Detail area is where the application may display information associated with the team. An empty state in a reference screenshot does not prove that a current account has no members, and it does not establish why a particular entry is missing. Review any filters, dates or status explanations available in your signed-in screen. Before relying on a commission amount, read the applicable conditions, calculation method, processing times and any restrictions shown by the service.

Keep team assistance separate from account access

Helping someone find a registration guide does not require their password, verification code, recovery details or MPIN. Each person should create and control their own account credentials. If you share a link, describe where it leads and avoid presenting a screenshot’s balance or recharge amount as a prediction of someone else’s result. For account-specific problems, direct the account holder to the current support options in their own signed-in application rather than collecting their credentials.

Common questions

Is the screenshot invite link ready to use?

The displayed example includes an undefined code, so it should be treated as a reference to the layout. Copy and review the current invite link from your own account. The registration guide on this website explains the existing project registration destination separately.

Are Commission and Team Recharge the same?

They are different labels in the supplied Team screen. Their exact definitions, qualifying conditions and calculation rules should be checked in the current service. A public guide cannot determine the meaning of a particular account transaction from a screenshot alone.

Does the Team page guarantee rewards?

No. A dashboard layout and a screenshot do not guarantee income or a reward. Review current terms, eligibility, fees and records before relying on an amount. The task examples and calculator elsewhere on this website are arithmetic illustrations, not predictions of account results.
